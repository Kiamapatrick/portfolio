History Platform

A blockchain-based vehicle history system — storing servicing records, ownership history, accident reports and maintenance logs on an immutable ledger.

By storing vehicle records on blockchain infrastructure, the system ensures that vehicle information cannot be altered or manipulated — improving transparency and trust in the automotive industry between buyers, sellers and service providers.

Immutable Records

Vehicle history stored permanently on-chain — servicing, accidents and ownership changes that no one can alter.

Ownership Tracking

Transparent, verifiable chain of ownership from manufacture to present day — instantly provable by any buyer.

Verified Maintenance

Service providers add maintenance records directly to the blockchain — eliminating fake or forged service histories.
